About the odds we show

Two of the names in our odds columns are platforms rather than bookmakers.

Altenar

Altenar supplies the sportsbook software and the pricing that other betting brands run on. Same idea as Kambi: the operator puts their own name on the front and the odds come from Altenar. Any odds we show from Altenar are the same odds shown at every bookmaker using it.

Kambi

Kambi prices the markets for hundreds of bookmakers worldwide. Any odds we show from Kambi are the same odds shown at every bookmaker running their platform, so check whether yours is one of them.

How to use the trends

Read the section, then narrow with filters until you find the edge.

Every trend shown here has already passed a consistency threshold. Players and teams appear because they have hit a statistical line repeatedly across their recent matches, not occasionally. The default view is intentionally broad so you can see what is available, and the filters are where you narrow it down to find a genuine edge.

Filter sidebar vs filter tabs
The sidebar lets you filter by the quality of the trend itself: minimum hit rate, minimum odds, and how many matches the threshold needs to have been cleared. The tabs at the top work differently; they change which type of trend you are looking at entirely. Use both together to zero in on exactly what you want.
All competitions vs same league only
You can analyse a player or team's history using all of their matches across every competition, or limit it to matches from the same league as their upcoming fixture. All competitions gives you a larger sample to work from. Same league only gives you more specific context. If a player has limited cup or European minutes, same league only is usually the cleaner read.
Understanding venue filters
Trends can be home and away mixed, or specific to one venue type. By default you see everything. Selecting "any venue" shows only trends that hold regardless of home or away. Selecting "venue specific" shows trends that only exist at one venue, which tend to be stronger signals when they appear because the pattern is tighter.
What the arrows mean
An arrow next to a name shows whether that player or team is averaging more or less of the stat in their most recent matches compared to earlier ones. Green means the momentum is working in favour of the bet. Red means it is working against it. A trend with a strong hit rate and a green arrow is the combination worth focusing on.
